Use case · Context

Recruiters screen many CVs for every offer

Users

HR recruiters and hiring managers

Input

One job offer and several CVs, as PDFs with different layouts

Output

A skills comparison with evidence quotes and the missing requirements

Decision

Always made by a human

Why current tools fall short

  • Keyword filters miss synonyms (“JS” vs JavaScript)
  • Two-column or scanned CVs lose their text
  • LLM summaries sound sure of themselves but cite no proof
  • Result: good profiles missed and unequal treatment
