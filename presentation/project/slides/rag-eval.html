Evaluation plan

  • 30 test queries, e.g. “Has the candidate used Kubernetes?”
  • Relevant chunks labelled by 2 annotators
  • Metrics: Recall@5, MRR, nDCG@5
  • Chunking A (sections) vs B (recursive 500/50)
  • Failures documented, not hidden
