Use case · Scope

An MVP that supports the decision but never makes it

In scope

  • Read PDF CVs of any layout, scans included
  • Split the job offer into must-have and nice-to-have requirements
  • For each requirement: met, partial or missing
  • A quote and page number for every claim
  • Audit pass that flags unsupported claims
  • Remove personal details before any LLM call

Out of scope

  • Automatic rejection or hiring
  • Video interviews, emotion analysis
  • Integration with an HR system (ATS)
  • Any use of age, gender, photo or origin

SmartRecruit · Week 1 check-in · 4

Recruitment is a high-risk use case under the EU AI Act, so the human decision and the audit trail are part of the design, not extras.
